My Top 5 Favorite Movies

Hey! I'm Nathan and these are my top five favorite movies. I've always been really into fantasy, mythology, and movies that take you into a completely different world. Picking only five was actually harder than I thought, but these are the ones I always come back to.

  1. The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King

    This one will probably always be my favorite. I love everything about Middle-earth, and seeing everyone's stories finally come together is so satisfying. The battles, friendships, and ending are just amazing. I'm also really excited for the new season of The Rings of Power!

  2. Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows: Part 2

    I grew up loving the Harry Potter movies, so this one has a special place for me. It's honestly so nostalgic, and I love how everything builds up to the final battle at Hogwarts. I'm actually reading the fifth book right now!

  3. The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug

    I could honestly watch movies set in Middle-earth all day. I love the adventure, the different locations, and especially Smaug. The whole dragon scene is easily one of my favorite parts. I actually own all of the books and more!

  4. Troy

    I've always thought Greek mythology was really interesting, which is a big reason I love this movie. I'm also a big Brad Pitt fan! The characters, battles, and drama make it so entertaining, even when you already know how the story ends.

  5. Avatar

    One of my favorite things about Avatar is how beautiful Pandora is. I love all the plants, animals, and the connection to nature. The world feels so alive, and I think that's what makes the movie so special to me, especially in 3D!
